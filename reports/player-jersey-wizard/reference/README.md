# Jersey reconstruction reference evidence

Evidence was saved before procedural jersey implementation. The runtime result is an original,
stylized NOVA COURT uniform informed by the listed dimensions and construction cues; it is not a
licensed Nike product or a pixel-identical garment reconstruction.

## Primary multi-angle silhouette evidence

- `nike-jersey-04.png` — Nike Team Sports men's basketball catalog, Digital Pinnacle Legend
  jersey page. It provides unobstructed front and back three-quarter product views and identifies
  the garment as lightweight 100% polyester with a tailored performance fit.
- `nike-jersey-06.png` — Nike Team Sports "Unlimited Digital Jersey" overview. It provides
  front, right-side, and back orthographic-style views of the same sleeveless performance
  silhouette, including the armhole depth, shoulder straps, waist drape, and straight lower hem.
- The selected catalog pages above are retained locally; the complete source catalog remains
  available from Nike Team Sports:
  <https://niketeam.nike.com/niketeamsports/content/pdf/catalog_thumbs/NTS_M_Basketball.pdf?cb=20200915>

## Measurement evidence

- The catalog's Digital Pinnacle Legend page lists a size-L body width of 20.75 inches and body
  length of 29 inches, with +1.25-inch and +2.75-inch length options. The task's requested
  reconstruction contract uses its supplied 19-inch width and 29.75-inch length target for the
  authored medium/large hybrid game silhouette.
- Nike's NBA/WNBA jersey chart maps size L to a 41–44-inch body chest and documents +2-inch
  tall sizing. Source: <https://www.nike.com/size-fit/nba-wnba-jerseys>
- `nfhs-1.png`, `nfhs-3.png`, and `nfhs-4.png` preserve the NFHS front, back, and side uniform
  diagrams. They specify a front number at least 4 inches tall, back number at least 6 inches
  tall, armhole trim no wider than 1 inch, and a centered side insert no wider than 4 inches.
  Source: <https://assets.nfhs.org/umbraco/media/7213102/nfhs-basketball-uniforms_final.pdf>

## Observed construction

- Macro silhouette: sleeveless long torso shell, shallow V neck, narrow shoulder bridges, deep
  armholes, and a loose hem that hangs below the waistband.
- Meso construction: front/back panels joined at the side seam; contrast neckline and armhole
  binding; narrow side insert; lower hem with a subtle outward flare.
- Micro/material response: fine polyester knit reads matte with a low-amplitude highlight;
  folds concentrate below the chest and near the side seams rather than deforming the shoulder
  anchors.
- Hidden/inferred regions: exact interior seams, back-neck construction, cloth thickness, and
  under-arm panel topology are not fully recoverable from the catalog images. Those regions use
  symmetrical low-poly procedural construction and are labeled approximations.
