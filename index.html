HOW TO PLAY

MOVEWASD

Use the left stick on gamepad. Sprint with Shift / RT.

SHOOTSPACE

Hold to rise and release while the curved meter tip is inside the green window. A green release is perfect.

HANDLEQ + MOVE

Basic: hesi, crossover, between-the-legs, behind-back, or double-tap in-and-out. Hold Shift for spin, double-cross, snatch-back, and push-cross.

PASSE

Hit the open teammate and instantly switch control to the receiver.

DEFENDSPACE / I

Contest or block with Space. Reach with I or E; steals knock the ball live and mistimed reaches can stun you.

FINISHI NEAR RIM

Hold I in the dunk zone and release in green for a perfect power finish. I remains steal on defense.

CAMERAC

Cycle follow, broadcast, and cinematic views at any time.
